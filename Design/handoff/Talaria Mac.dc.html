1Talaria for Mac — three window structures for the chat workspace
Context from the repo (howhowdg/talaria). Palette: Volt #CEFA45 for prominent fills with Graphite #181E17 text; Deep green #3F5B0A as light-mode accent; volt as dark-mode accent. Brand rule: keep Liquid Glass in the control layer (toolbars, buttons, floating composer) and quiet surfaces behind messages. Existing UI is a NavigationSplitView with sidebar → sessions, capsule glass buttons, a 26pt-radius glass composer.
Assumptions. Screens are 1180×760 Mac windows, system font, 13px body. Tool cards come from the desktop audit (grouped tools, reasoning disclosure with timer, approval stack, status stack: todos / background processes / subagents). Icons are placeholders — SF Symbols in the build.
Three directions. 1a stays inside the brand rule (glass only on controls). 1b adds Safari-style session tabs and a slide-in files panel — the most Hermes-Desktop-like. 1c pushes furthest: every region floats as a glass panel over the desktop wallpaper.
